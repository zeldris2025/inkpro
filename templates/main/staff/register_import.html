From Excel

Open the register, then File → Save As → CSV UTF-8, and upload that file. The title, totals and blank rows at the top are skipped automatically.

Safe to re-import

Entries already in the register are matched — by invoice number, or by date, client, job and amount — and updated, never duplicated. Import the same sheet each week and only new payments and new jobs change.

Balance and payment status are calculated from the amounts, so a mistake in the sheet’s own formulas can’t carry over.

Columns

Date, Client, Recharge / Job Details, Qty, Invoice No., Invoice Amount, Receipt No., Amount Received, Payment Method, Notes — and optionally Email, for payment reminders.
